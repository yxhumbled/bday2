Dear Nour,

You know we talk more than either of us probably admits out loud — so it felt strange to just send you a text today of all days. This one gets an actual page.

You've always had this thing where you just know what you want and go get it — confident in a way that isn't loud about it, it's just there. Clever enough to make it look easy. And somehow you make even the most basic outfit look like it took effort when I know it took you five minutes.

Half the time I picture you, you're either in the water or with a pencil in your hand — swimming like it's the easiest thing in the world, or drawing something out of nothing. Add blue to that (obviously), a beach somewhere, and a plate of something annoyingly healthy, and that's just... you, basically.

But if I had to pick one thing to replay, it's the school trips. Not even one specific moment — just the whole chaos of it, the two of us finding each other in the middle of it every time. Those are still some of my favorite memories, easily.

So here's to another year of you being exactly this — a little too confident, a little too clever, and somehow still finding time to text me back immediately. Happy birthday.
